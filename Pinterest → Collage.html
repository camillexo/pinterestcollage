Pinterest → Collage

A one-click button for your bookmarks bar. Open any Pinterest board or section, click it, and get a randomized collage you can shuffle and download as a PNG. Works on secret boards too, because it runs in your own signed-in browser.

1 · Install (once)

Show your bookmarks bar (⌘ShiftB on Mac, CtrlShiftB on Windows), then drag this red button onto it:

🧩 Pin Collage

If dragging doesn't work: click Copy code instead, right-click your bookmarks bar → Add page…, name it “Pin Collage”, and paste into the URL field.

2 · Use it on any board

  1. Sign in to Pinterest in that browser and open a board or a section, such as pinterest.com/camilleminns_/fastforward/the-union/.
  2. Click 🧩 Pin Collage in your bookmarks bar. It scrolls the board to collect every pin, then opens the collage on top of the page.
  3. It opens in Scrapbook with Photos first. Pick a layout and size, then hit 🎲 Shuffle until you like it. Click ⬇ Download PNG to save it.

Good to know

Big boards

It scrolls up to about 120 screens, which covers several hundred pins. It starts at 48 pins, which suits Scrapbook. Change the Pins box for more or fewer. Each shuffle picks a new random set.

Photos first: how text images are handled

The tool spots pins that are mostly words (quotes, affirmations, graphics) by looking for lines of type, and the bar shows how many it found. Photos first fills the collage with photos and uses text pins only if it runs out. Leave out drops them entirely, and Mix in treats everything the same. Text printed over a busy photo can slip through. Lower the Pins number to keep only the most photo-like ones.

It stops at “More ideas”

Pinterest's suggested pins under your board are left out, so only your saved pins appear.

Browsers

Built and tested for Chrome. Edge, Arc and Brave also run bookmark buttons like this. It doesn't run in the Pinterest phone app.

Privacy

Everything happens in your browser tab. Nothing is uploaded or sent anywhere.
